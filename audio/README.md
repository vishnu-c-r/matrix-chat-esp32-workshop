# The Matrix Movie Audio Clips Directory

You can drop authentic audio clips (.mp3 files) into this folder to replace the browser's speech synthesis with the original actors' voices (Hugo Weaving as Agent Smith, Laurence Fishburne as Morpheus, and Carrie-Anne Moss as Trinity)!

### Supported Audio Filenames:
- `morpheus_pill.mp3` -> Slide 0 (Red Pill / Blue Pill monologue)
- `morpheus_lie.mp3` -> Slide 1 ("What if I told you that everything you know was a lie?")
- `morpheus_truth.mp3` -> Slide 8 ("All I am offering is the truth. Nothing more.")
- `morpheus_door.mp3` -> Slide 25 ("I can only show you the door. You have to walk through it.")
- `morpheus_free_mind.mp3` -> Slide 27 ("Free your mind. Enter the Matrix.")
- `trinity_tapped.mp3` -> Slide 28 ("Neo, the hardline is tapped! They know you are here.")
- `smith_escape.mp3` -> Slide 29 ("Did you really think you could escape, Mr. Anderson?")
- `smith_cure.mp3` -> Slide 30 ("You are a disease, Mr. Anderson. And we are the cure.")
- `smith_machine.mp3` -> Slide 33 ("Never send a human to do a machine's job.")
- `smith_hide.mp3` -> Slide 36 ("You can run... but you cannot hide.")
- `smith_inevitability.mp3` -> Slide 37 ("That is the sound of inevitability... Goodbye, Mr. Anderson.")

*Note: If an MP3 file is not present in this folder, the presentation automatically uses the browser's natural neural speech synthesizer with operator radio comms effects.*
