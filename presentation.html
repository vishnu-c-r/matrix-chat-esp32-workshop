The Red Pill

Today, we are building a completely off-grid, decentralised chat network.

  • No Routers.
  • No Internet.
  • No Central Server.

Your ESP32-C3 board will act as both the server and the client simultaneously.

The Protocol: ESP-NOW

How do we communicate without a router?

  • Connectionless: Peer-to-peer Wi-Fi protocol developed by Espressif.
  • Fast: Extremely low latency compared to standard Wi-Fi.
  • Direct: Devices talk directly to each other using MAC addresses.

The Architecture

  • The Backend: FreeRTOS handles incoming radio packets asynchronously.
  • The UI: A built-in WebServer hosting an embedded HTML/JS Captive Portal.
  • The Hardware: ESP32-C3 processing everything locally in your pocket.

Your Mission

  • Open the codebase and navigate to include/config.h
  • Set your unique Node ID (1 to 50) and your Chat Name.
  • Click Upload in PlatformIO.
  • Connect to your board's Wi-Fi network (e.g., NEO-14) from your phone.
  • Infiltrate the network and start chatting!
